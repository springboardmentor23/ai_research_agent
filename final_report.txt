## Abstract

The field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) has witnessed a paradigm shift with the advent of deep learning techniques, offering potential solutions to longstanding challenges in areas such as machine translation, sentiment analysis, and text summarization. Given the rapid evolution of this domain, a comparative analysis of various deep learning architectures is crucial to understanding their relative strengths and weaknesses. This review aims to synthesize current research on the performance of different deep learning models in NLP tasks, with a specific focus on the transformative impact of transformer-based architectures. The reviewed studies employ a range of methodologies, including empirical evaluations on benchmark datasets, ablation studies to assess the contribution of individual components, and comparative analyses against traditional statistical NLP methods. The findings suggest that transformer architectures represent a significant advancement in NLP, offering substantial performance gains over previous approaches.
